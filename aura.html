Breathe Fresh

A fresh-smelling space, all day long.

Aura's passive scenting keeps fragrance strength constant 24 hours a day. The wall-mounted dispenser suits everything from a small business restroom to a large commercial space. Visitors stay comfortable, and your business makes a better impression.

The fragrance refills are VOC compliant in the U.S. and were tested thoroughly before launch. From the Aura app you schedule daily, weekly or interval scenting, adjust the output precisely, and watch over the dispenser all day.
