About Switchers

Turning ideas into fully functional products.

Switchers is a California-based engineering company specializing in intelligent electronic product development. We at Switchers combine electronics engineering, embedded systems, wireless communication, sensor integration, and IoT technologies to create connected products that solve real-world business challenges.

In addition to delivering isolated hardware or software components, we develop integrated solutions where hardware, firmware, connectivity, and applications work together from concept and architecture through prototyping, testing, manufacturing support, and deployment.

Established in 2014, Switchers has deployed more than 50,000 sensor-integrated devices of various types for automated field data collection and processing.

Process

From early idea to fully functional product.

A focused engineering path keeps decisions visible, prototypes useful, and production handoff grounded in tested hardware, firmware and application software.

01

Discover

Clarify product goals, system constraints, key components, connectivity needs, and success criteria.

02

Architect

Define electronics architecture, firmware approach, sensing strategy, and integration plan.

03

Prototype

Build functional boards, embedded software, connectivity flows, and early product behaviors.

04

Validate

Test performance, reliability, power behavior, wireless stability, regulatory compliances and certification and manufacturability risks.

05

Launch

Support production handoff with documentation, revisions, supplier coordination, and deployment readiness.
