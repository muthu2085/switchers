Who We Are

We build complete systems, not just isolated parts.

Established in 2014, Switchers is a California-based engineering company specializing in intelligent electronic product development. We combine electronics engineering, embedded systems, wireless communication, sensor integration, and IoT technologies to create connected products that solve real-world business challenges.

In addition to delivering isolated hardware or software components, we develop integrated solutions where hardware, firmware, connectivity, and applications work together from concept and architecture through prototyping, testing, manufacturing support, and deployment.

Our team has extensive experience in embedded electronics, wireless communication, and IoT systems. We have successfully deployed over 50,000 sensor-integrated wireless devices across various industries, enabling automated field data collection and processing for our clients.
